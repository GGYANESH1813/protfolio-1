# Portfolio Website PRD

## Audience
Technical recruiters, engineering leads, hiring managers, and interviewers who want to evaluate projects, foundational computer science capabilities, and AI/ML competencies for internship opportunities.

## Main Goals
1. **Explain Identity Quickly:** Immediately articulate who G Gyanesh is (1st-year B.Tech Computer Science student specializing in AI/ML at GIET University Gunupur).
